PACA Trust — Under the Perishable Agricultural Commodities Act, all proceeds and accounts receivable from unpaid produce sales are held in a statutory trust for the benefit of the seller (and its lender by assignment), with priority ahead of all other secured creditors. This gives the facility super-priority collateral that supersedes Article 9 UCC liens.
